# Privacy Policy for Arrowverse: Maze Puzzle
**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

ProteusLabs operates the game Arrowverse: Maze Puzzle. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our game.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Arrowverse does not require you to create an account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information (such as your real name, email address, or phone number).

To support basic gameplay features, analytics, and advertising, we and third-party service providers may process:
- **Device Identifiers:** An anonymous unique device ID or instance ID used locally or for leaderboard score submission.
- **Gameplay Data:** Completed levels, high scores, and in-game shop cosmetic unlock states (stored locally on your device).

### 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising
Our application integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:
- **Google Play Services:** Used for app integrity, distribution, and optional testing tracks.
- **Google AdMob:** Used to serve advertisements (such as rewarded ads or interstitial banners). AdMob may use device advertising IDs (GAID) and cookies to serve relevant ads according to Google's policies.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 3. Children's Privacy
Arrowverse is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided personal data, contact us so we can promptly delete it.

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Most game progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game configurations and scores.
